Why Ari

Somebody had to say it

I could just say she's beautiful and stop there. It would be true. Ask anybody who's met her. But that's the easy part, and honestly it's not even the part that gets me. It's how she carries it. Like she decided a long time ago that she belongs wherever she's standing, and nobody's ever proven her wrong.

Drop her anywhere. New city, new job, a room full of strangers. Doesn't matter. In every garden, she flowers. Most people need things to go right before they can be themselves. Ari brings her own weather.

And here's the thing about her light. It doesn't stay on her. You watch people around her start laughing more, talking more, acting like better versions of themselves and not even noticing they're doing it. I noticed. That's why this exists.

Why her, and only her

Solo ella

I've been a lot of places. Forty-three states, a few countries, more rooms than I can count. You meet a lot of people living like that, and after a while you learn the difference between someone you notice and someone you can't stop noticing. She's the second kind. It isn't close.

Ask me why her and the honest answer sounds simple. Nobody else ever made me want to learn the small stuff. How she takes her coffee. What song fixes her bad days. Why she laughs at the exact things she laughs at. That kind of curiosity doesn't show up on command. With her it showed up on day one and never left.

So no, there's no list this is pulled from. No plan B sitting somewhere. Un jardín entero, y una sola flor que me importa. That's the whole answer, and I didn't have to think about it.

La Invitación

Written by hand, meant for one reader

L·A

Houston · Written on a Thursday, thinking about Wednesday

Ari,

There's a museum in this city full of things people crossed oceans to see. I've walked past it a hundred times. I never had a reason to go in until you.

So here it is, in writing, where I can't take it back: come with me this Wednesday. Marble halls, quiet rooms, paintings older than both our families. Everything in that building was made to be admired — and I'm telling you now, none of it will get my full attention. You'll be the most precious thing in every room and I want to see that with my own eyes.

Choose how we do it. Then answer me down below, where it counts.

— Luis M.
